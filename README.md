## Feedback, bugs and ideas

Use the **[Feedback board](../../discussions)** (GitHub Discussions):

- **Q&A**: bugs and questions - tell me the mod name, what you saw on screen, and attach the mod's `.log` file if it has one
- **Ideas**: what you would change or add
- **Show and tell**: share your screenshots and videos made with the mods

A free GitHub account is enough to post. You can also comment on the Nexus page or under the YouTube video. I read all of them.
